About the school

A girls' secondary school in Morulem

Morulem Girls' Senior Secondary School is a Ugandan secondary school located in Morulem in Abim District. Public education records identify it as a government-aided school under Uganda's Ministry of Education and Sports.

The Ministry of Education's 2025 Post-Primary Selection Information Guide lists the school as a universal government-aided secondary school, with O-Level and A-Level education and a girls/boarding category.

The school is also listed by the Diocese of Kotido among its Catholic schools, reflecting the institution's connection with the wider Morulem mission and Catholic education environment.

Information policy: This profile intentionally does not publish school fees, a headteacher's name, a school motto, admission dates, subject combinations, or examination-performance claims unless those details can be reliably verified from public records.

Facilities and current conditions

Facility information is presented carefully because conditions can change and the most recent independent inspection identified areas requiring attention.

Documented facilities

  • A 2020/2021-era education profile reported 10 classrooms.
  • The same profile reported 2 laboratories.
  • The profile reported 10 latrine stances.
  • The school has participated in science and innovation activities at regional level.

Infrastructure requiring attention

  • The Office of the Auditor General's 2025 physical inspection described the girls' dormitories as dilapidated.
  • The same audit reported that about 50% of the school structures were on borrowed territory intended for a Catholic university.
  • These findings reflect the audit inspection in October 2025 and should not be interpreted as a guarantee of the present condition of every building.
Important: Infrastructure information on this page is deliberately transparent. The 2025 audit is an independent government record and is included so families can make informed decisions rather than presenting an unrealistically perfect picture of the school.
